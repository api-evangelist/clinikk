# Clinikk

> Clinikk is a Bangalore, India-based primary healthcare provider offering membership-based access to unlimited doctor consultations, free prescribed medicines and lab tests, 24/7 teleconsultation, and optional group health insurance. Clinikk operates in-person Health Hubs across Bangalore alongside a consumer mobile app. It does not publish a public developer API.

## About
- Company: Clinikk (Clinikk Health Hub)
- Industry: Healthcare / Primary Care + Health Insurance
- Headquarters: Bengaluru, Karnataka, India
- Model: Membership plans (from ~Rs 900/month) covering OPD visits, medicines, lab tests, specialist referrals, and cashless hospitalization; optional group health insurance in partnership with IndusInd General Insurance.
- Backed by: 500 Global (portfolio company)
